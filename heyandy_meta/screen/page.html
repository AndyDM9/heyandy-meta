Keeping an eye on

What can I ask Andy?

Start with “Andy”. These are ideas to say out loud.

Find things

“Andy, where are my glasses?”

“Andy, the blue mug, next to the laptop.”

“Andy, I lost my keys in the kitchen.”

Remember things

“Andy, keep an eye on my keys.”

“Andy, what do you remember?”

“Andy, remind me at 3 pm to call Sam.”

Talk and look up

“Andy, how are you?”

“Andy, what's the weather tomorrow?”

“Andy, forget my keys.”

# The TV beside Andy

**Andy** is a tabletop robot (a Reachy Mini Lite, for now) that finds what you've lost and points at it, knows your
own mug from anyone else's, keeps an eye on the things you choose, checks you have them when you go out, reminds you in
person, plays the cup game, shows the web on the TV, and speaks Spanish. It was built by **HeyAndy, Inc.** for Meta's Global AI Developer Hackathon (startup track, *Push the
Frontier*), on Meta's models: Muse Glimmer, SAM 3.1 (with Object Multiplex), DINOv2 and Omnilingual ASR from their open
weights on the computer beside the robot, and Muse Spark and Muse Image through the Meta Model API.

This page is the robot's TV, running the same page and server as on the robot, **on sample data**: no robot, camera,
models or keys are here, and the picture is drawn, not taken in a home. Pick a scene at the top: what Andy shows while
it searches, what it found (with SAM 3.1's outline), your own mug told from another, the cup game (every cup's path
through the shuffle), going out, Andy in Spanish, a web page Andy opened, an answer with sources, a picture, what it remembers, and "What can I
ask?". The web page in "On the web" is Wikipedia's (CC BY-SA 4.0).
Nothing you do here reaches anyone else.

In a real home, pictures and voice never leave the house: SAM 3.1, Muse Glimmer and the speech models run on the
computer beside the robot, and Muse Spark only ever gets words (and, when you ask Andy to show something on the TV,
screenshots of that web page).

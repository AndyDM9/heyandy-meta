# The TV beside Andy

**Andy** is a tabletop robot (a Reachy Mini Lite, for now) that finds what you've lost and points at it, keeps an eye
on the things you choose, reminds you in person, and answers with sources. It was built by **HeyAndy, Inc.** for Meta's
Global AI Developer Hackathon (startup track, *Push the Frontier*), on Meta's models: Muse Glimmer and SAM 3.1 from their
open weights and Omnilingual ASR on the computer beside the robot, and Muse Spark through the Meta Model API.

This page is the robot's TV, running the same page and server as on the robot, **on sample data**: no robot, camera,
models or keys are here, and the picture is drawn, not taken in a home. Pick a scene at the top: what Andy shows while
it searches, what it found (with SAM 3.1's outline), an answer with sources, what it remembers, and "What can I ask?".
Nothing you do here reaches anyone else.

In a real home, pictures and voice never leave the house: SAM 3.1, Muse Glimmer and the speech models run on the
computer beside the robot, and Muse Spark only ever gets words.

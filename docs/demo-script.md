# The 3-minute video (draft)

Judged on impact (30%), use of Meta's models (25%), technical depth (20%) and the demo (25%). One uncut take carries
the demo; the numbers carry the depth. Filmed on the Reachy Mini Lite in a staged room (no other people, nothing
personal in view). The TV page is screen-recorded at the same time.

| Time | On screen | Said (voice-over or Andy) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Title card | **Required first line (FAQ):** "Before this week we had a Reachy Mini and an earlier object finder built before this event; everything you'll see was built this week, on Meta's models." *(the founder's exact words to be decided)* |
| 0:06–0:20 | A person looking for their glasses | The problem: misplaced things, every day, for people who live at home. A robot that looks for you and points. |
| 0:20–1:05 | **The uncut take**, robot and TV side by side, badge "autonomous · uncut · 1x speed" | "Andy, where are my glasses?" Andy turns to the voice, says "Let me look", sweeps, skips the glasses *on the TV*, looks closer, points: "I see your glasses, to my right." The TV shows the frame with SAM 3.1's mask, the plan, the timer, and the model strip. |
| 1:05–1:30 | Second take | "Andy, the blue mug, next to the laptop." Glimmer compares the two mugs on the computer and points at the right one. |
| 1:30–1:50 | Network cable pulled; TV shows "Offline" | It still works: everything that sees and hears runs on the computer beside the robot. "Pictures and voice stay in your home." |
| 1:50–2:15 | Reminder take | "Remind me in a minute to take the bins out." A minute later Andy finds the person, turns to them, and says it. |
| 2:15–2:40 | The scorecard (sim and physical), the open-weights check | The numbers, failures included. Every Meta model and where it runs: Muse Glimmer, SAM 3.1, Omnilingual ASR on our hardware; Muse Spark in the cloud for words only. |
| 2:40–3:00 | Robot close-up | What's next (our own robot design, as a silhouette only), and the one-line ask. |

**Rules for filming:** no Meta logos; no music we don't own; subtitles burned in; no condition names and no health,
safety or loneliness claims; the people Andy is for are "people who live at home"; our own robot design is never
shown beyond a silhouette.

**For X (Most Viral):** the uncut take cut to 30 seconds, captions burned in, the same badge, posted after we submit.
